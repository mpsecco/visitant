# Visitant Demo

- Start with the problem: demos are harder when your eyes keep leaving the thing you are showing. [20s]
- Visitant gives you a small script overlay that stays visible to you. [12s]
- The overlay is click-through, so you can keep interacting with the app underneath it. [15s]
- Press Cmd-Option-M to enter move mode, drag or resize the overlay, then press CMD-Option-M again. [20s]
- Adjust transparency from the menu bar until the transcript is readable but unobtrusive. [15s]
- A transcript is just a Markdown file. Timed steps use markers like [3s] or [1m]. [18s]
- Untimed steps wait until you press Cmd-Option-Right.
- Use Cmd-Option-Space to start or pause automatic playback. [10s]
- Use Cmd-Option-H to hide the overlay when you do not need it. [8s]
- For Chrome tab recordings, the overlay appears on your screen but not in the captured tab. [18s]
- For whole-screen capture, the capture exclusion toggle is best-effort, so test your setup first. [18s]
- That is the core loop: write a short script, rehearse less, and keep your attention where the demo is happening.
